Symfony applications, in the browser

These applications run on PHP, compiled to WebAssembly: in your tab, with their database. No server runs them, what you change stays in your browser. Each one downloads about 25 MB, once.

  • The Symfony Demo

    The official demo, as it is: a blog with its admin, Doctrine and SQLite, Stimulus and 30 languages.

  • EasyAdmin

    The back office of a record shop that never existed: a sales dashboard, filters, batch actions, and fixtures loaded in your browser.

Built with PHP WASM Builder (source).
